# M0 human gate: drifting clusters

Desired motion: **loose, drifting clusters that gather and stretch under the lantern**. No preset is approved yet. Automated checks establish correctness boundaries only.

## First pass (about ten minutes)

Keep the seed, fixture and lantern settings identical when comparing presets. Reset before each trial. Start with the three-creature fixture so you can distinguish individual responses; use the full 24 only after that is readable.

1. **Gather and carry, blue only.** Approach, let the three settle, then lead them about 8–10 metres. Move faster briefly and slow down again. Look for a readable stretch and recovery, with individual drift still visible. Record whether blue vacuums them, immobilizes them, or leaves them endlessly behind.
2. **Turn and release.** Guide around a trunk into the return region. First lead with blue; reset and compare orange pressure from behind or the flank. The simpler blue solution is allowed. Record any snag, hidden influence through a trunk, confusing footprint, or irrecoverable scatter.
3. **Recover a split.** Deliberately lose part of a group around a trunk, close the shutter, then recover it. Record whether you can predict who will respond without staring at the debug panel. There is no stationary mushroom in M0; resting-source tests belong to a later authorized addition.

Do this first with plain boids, then arousal memory, then independent seekers. For a quick first comparison, repeat only gather/carry with all three and try the other situations on the promising pair. Do not tune everything between trials.

## Bounded tuning

Change only one dimension at a time: social strength, wandering, lamp strength, or arousal recovery. Use the actual parameter names exposed in the panel. Save a named preset before changing another dimension.

- Too much synchronized motion: reduce alignment before removing all social behavior.
- Blue collects a tight ball: examine arrival distance/separation and lamp strength before adding new states.
- Orange causes recovery work rather than useful pressure: reduce noise/strength before adding mechanics.
- Arousal feels unpredictable: shorten memory, compare the immediate baseline, and keep memory only if the difference is pleasant and legible.

These are design hypotheses, not experimentally established findings. See `research/` for the literature and its limits.
