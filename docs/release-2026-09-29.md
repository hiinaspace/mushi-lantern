## Validation and review

- Both ZIPs passed integrity checks. The final PCKs contain no screenshot or
  its imported texture; no extra native binaries are embedded in the PCKs.
- Linux passed its explicit inventory/ABI audit, Ubuntu 22.04 loading and ONNX
  inference, and natto Ubuntu Vulkan rendering with `/nix` hidden. The final
  archive was rendered again after the screenshot exclusion.
- Windows started headlessly under Wine, rendered the grove through Vulkan,
  captured a screenshot and exited 0. The final `play-desktop.bat` was also
  exercised through Wine `cmd`, rendered, captured and exited 0. Wine logged
  its thread-affinity/Mesa probing warnings; these are not native Windows
  or headset qualification.
- Clean-context review approved the Linux layout. A separate clean-context
  Windows reviewer found two issues: the unwanted screenshot, and VC++ runtime
  documentation that omitted ONNX. Both were corrected. The reviewer verified
  both final ZIP hashes, screenshot absence in both PCKs, unchanged Windows
  PE binaries, and no remaining static packaging blockers.
- Actual Windows hardware/OpenXR, audible spatial/voice quality, and WAN
  behavior were not newly qualified by this packaging refresh.
