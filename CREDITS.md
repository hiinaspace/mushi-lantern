# Credits

## Audio recordings

The following recordings were shared under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
We credit their creators and keep source links even though the license does not
require attribution. The game uses edited mono excerpts; exact source hashes,
timings, and processing are in [the field audio notes](assets/audio/field/README.md).

| Creator | Recording | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OwlStorm (Ashe Kirk / Owlish Media) | [Night Crickets Ambience on Rural Property](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/320145/) | Localized night insects |
| kyles | [Crickets Night Forest with 2 Sharp Close Cicadas Back and Forth](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/453862/) | Localized cicadas |
| Dominik_W | [Footsteps Walking on Foliage in a Forest](https://freesound.org/people/Dominik_W/sounds/398685/) | Walking on leaves and brush |
| happy_boozer | [Lantern Swinging](https://freesound.org/people/happy_boozer/sounds/411530/) | Lantern movement |
| ImAFoley | [Oil Lantern Open and Close](https://freesound.org/people/ImAFoley/sounds/516740/) | Shutter movement and randomized filter adjustment transients |
| deadrobotmusic | [Wooden Wick Candle Crackle 1](https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/622854/) | Lantern flame texture |

Mushi calls and remaining temporary cues are generated by the project scripts.
Source generation and these recordings' edits are documented alongside the
assets. The open and close excerpts were accepted after listening review;
three additional source transients are available as filter cue variations.
