# Lantern material sources

- Brass casing and trim: [Brass by Share Textures](https://cc0-textures.com/t/st-brass), downloaded as the official 4K ZIP. Source license: public domain / CC0. The 1K albedo, normal, and roughness JPEGs here are downscaled from the original archive with FFmpeg; height and metallic maps were omitted to keep the in-game material small. The casing uses the material at two tints for the dark frame and polished detail.
- Lantern panel detail: [Paper 002 by CC0 Textures](https://cc0-textures.com/t/cc0t-paper-002), downloaded as the official 4K JPG ZIP. Source license: public domain / CC0. The 1K albedo JPEG here is downscaled from the original archive with FFmpeg and used only as subtle modulation under the existing colored window shader.

The full downloaded archives remain outside the project and are not included in game exports.
