# Field audio sources

These edited mono PCM WAVs are from Freesound recordings published under [Creative Commons Zero](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Their original creators and pages are recorded here even though CC0 does not require attribution. The source downloads are kept outside the repository; run `python scripts/process_field_audio.py` with the six original files in `~/Downloads` to rebuild the game assets. The script checks each source's SHA-256 and uses FFmpeg 9.0.1 to downmix to mono, resample to 48 kHz, apply conservative gain and short edge fades, and encode 16-bit PCM.

The shutter recording contains several separate gestures. The open, close, and variant C cuts start just before their main movement transients so the audio lands promptly with the control motion. Three additional short transient groups are available as side-to-side filter variations; rotate among them to reduce repetition. They were selected from the source waveform and remain candidates for listening review in context. The footsteps, insect beds, swing, and wick retain natural variation. Their edge fades avoid clicks, but a repeating player may reveal a quiet seam or rhythmic restart; a short crossfade or different trim can be chosen after listening in context.
