# Forest asset provenance

The Oak / Pine audition uses the same deterministic prop placement records, coarse collision, existing grass and rock meshes, and lighting. Pine is the headset-selected launch default. `MUSHI_FOREST_STYLE=oak` selects the broadleaf comparison; `MUSHI_FOREST_STYLE=procedural` preserves the generated tree and bush fallback. Mushroom fruit is the small pale woodland form documented below and is displayed as a fairy circle; gameplay attraction remains uniform across the existing patch radius.

The former OpenGameArt Warspawn 83-triangle CC0 model was removed from the runtime asset folder after replacement; its source details remain in `PROVENANCE-SOURCES.md`. The naturalistic replacement was authored in Blender because the inspected direct-CC0 alternative had only 148 triangles and the higher-detail Sketchfab pack had conflicting license metadata and required sign-in to download. The replacement keeps a naturally pale surface. A mushroom-specific shader selects fruits independently and combines the albedo-derived edge mask with a thin view-angle silhouette rim, preserving separated blue edge luminescence at maximum night vision without changing the forest shader's reveal behavior. The rendered comparison fixture checks both the lantern-lit fairy circle and a dark, lantern-off walking-height NV0/NV1 pair (`tests/mushroom_patch_visual.gd`).

MIT and CC0 license texts are included under `licenses/`. Forest Ground 01 and 04 are 1K JPGs with 2m and 3.2m source tile dimensions. The runtime normalizes both ground candidates and the procedural rim texture to 1024x1024 RGBA8 with mipmaps because Terrain3D stores layers in a shared texture atlas. No parallax, depth, or AO maps are used.

The branch albedo textures embedded in the EZ-Tree GLBs import as fully transparent images in Godot 4.7. Pine branches now use the sourced Pine Bark albedo and normal maps, with a restrained tint and normal strength to keep trunks readable under the lantern without becoming bright. Oak retains a solid rough-bark fallback because this detail pass targets the selected pine default.

Pine ground uses Forest Ground 06 as its dark soil base and Rock 01 for the autoshader's steep-slope layer. A low-coverage filtered mask mixes existing Forest Ground 04 into dry patches, using its 1K albedo without another atlas normal layer. The same two-channel mask makes mossy-rock lichen patches a little more common; their violet emission still requires strong night adaptation. A filtered half-metre coverage texture blends mossy-rock albedo on gentle ground, with a soft slope fade and clearance from the goal, spawn, mushroom clearings, east traversal route and direct start-to-goal corridor. Terrain3D's encoded control map remains base needles plus its automatic slope texturing; the higher-resolution mask handles moss transitions without changing height data or prop, collision or GPU obstacle placement. Boulders use the same rough, triplanar Rock 01 albedo and normal on one procedural 16-radial by 9-ring faceted mesh (~256 triangles); multi-frequency vertex displacement, deterministic nonuniform scale and broader yaw scatter create the shape variety. Close pine grass again uses the meadow clump with sparse high-night-vision violet flecks; the generated tuft remains as its far LOD. Weed Plant 02 is retained only in audition source files. Shrub 02 is smaller and uses Poly Haven's separate alpha map for cutout silhouettes and violet edge emission. Fern clump size now ranges from 0.62 to 2.0 times the source mesh. At 128m, the placement source now has 24,000 deterministic grass records (96,000 at 256m); high quality renders every fourth and low quality every eighth record, preserving a 2x density fallback. The Royal Fern understory remains at existing bush placements with stronger deterministic scale variation.

Forest foliage edge and grass-fleck emission use restrained violet (#a96bd6 family) so they read apart from blue sleeping mushi. Tree edges use a lower strength than the initial green pass; ground flecks stay patchy and activate only near maximum night vision. River-gold emission and natural forest albedo are unchanged.

Tree edge reveal now varies in 28-cell leaf UV space rather than coarse world-space crown cells. Screen derivatives blend both selection and reveal stagger toward their sparse means once those cells approach subpixel size, reducing head-motion shimmer while retaining the 0.90–0.99 adaptation thresholds.

The oak and pine models are normalized to about 9.5m tall. Terrain3D LOD distances are set at 32m / 80m / 144m for high vegetation and 20m / 55m / 115m for low vegetation. The farthest LOD remains visible through the 128m or 256m basin. These are desktop implementation defaults pending rendered performance and headset review.

The audition capture fixture uses identical framing and adaptation for both species:

```sh
XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/mushi-forest-oak-128 MUSHI_FOREST_STYLE=oak MUSHI_FOREST_CAPTURE=/tmp/mushi-forest-oak-128.png ./.local/godot/bin/godot4 --xr-mode off --path . --rendering-driver vulkan --rendering-method mobile --script tests/forest_audition_visual.gd -- --terrain-size 128
XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/mushi-forest-pine-128 MUSHI_FOREST_STYLE=pine MUSHI_FOREST_CAPTURE=/tmp/mushi-forest-pine-128.png ./.local/godot/bin/godot4 --xr-mode off --path . --rendering-driver vulkan --rendering-method mobile --script tests/forest_audition_visual.gd -- --terrain-size 128
```

Use distinct directories and set the final argument to `256` for the larger scene.
